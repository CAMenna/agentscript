Business Insurance

Coverage designed around how your business actually runs — not a generic policy template.

Coverage that follows your operations

Every business carries a different shape of risk depending on what it builds, sells, or serves. Our commercial lines team starts by mapping your operations — locations, equipment, contracts, and vendors — before recommending a single policy limit.

We place property, general liability, commercial auto, umbrella, and inland marine coverage through a broad panel of carriers, which means we're negotiating on your behalf rather than steering you toward a single insurer's product line.

What's typically included

Programs are built policy by policy, but most business insurance clients combine several of the following depending on size and industry.

  • Commercial property and business interruption coverage
  • General and product liability
  • Commercial auto and fleet programs
  • Umbrella and excess liability layers
  • Equipment breakdown and inland marine

How we work with you

Renewals aren't a once-a-year fire drill. Your advisory team checks in throughout the policy period, flags exposure changes as your business grows, and prepares a full marketing strategy well ahead of renewal so you're never negotiating from a deadline.

Home / Services / Risk Management

Risk Management

The goal isn't a bigger policy. It's fewer claims in the first place.

Prevention before transfer

Insurance transfers risk you couldn't prevent. Our risk consultants work upstream of that — identifying exposure in your operations, facilities, and supply chain before it turns into a loss.

What our consultants do

  • On-site safety and exposure assessments
  • Loss history analysis and trend reporting
  • Safety program and training design
  • Business continuity and disaster recovery planning
  • Contractual risk transfer review

Measured, not assumed

Every engagement ends with a report your leadership team can actually act on: prioritized findings, estimated cost of inaction, and a realistic timeline for fixing what matters most.

Claims Advocacy

When something goes wrong, you shouldn't have to fight your insurer alone.

An advocate, not a go-between

A claim is the moment your policy actually gets tested. Our claims advocates manage the process on your behalf — documentation, adjuster communication, and negotiation — so a stressful situation doesn't also become a confusing one.

How we help

  • First notice of loss filing and documentation support
  • Direct negotiation with carrier adjusters
  • Escalation for disputed or delayed claims
  • Post-claim policy review to close future gaps

Working for you, not the carrier

Because we're your broker, our incentive is a fair outcome for you — not a fast close for the insurer.
